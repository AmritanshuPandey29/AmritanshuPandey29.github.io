Data managers cross-check five systems by hand every week. I built an agent that does it: five MCP servers in Python over a synthetic trial in Databricks, with Claude deciding what to check next. It reads everything, but can only draft queries for a human to approve.

IQVIA · flagship GenAI initiativeDesigned & proposed

Patient-to-trial matching

A patient in Delhi types: "I have sugar, Metformin for six months, still high. Any trials?" The trial protocol says: Type 2 diabetes, HbA1c 7.5–10%, stable metformin 12+ weeks, eGFR above 60. Same facts, two languages. I designed a GenAI product that translates between them, helping patients find trials and sponsors find eligible patients near their sites.

1Understand both sidesLLM extraction turns the patient's words and the protocol's criteria into structured facts
2Find what matchesEmbedding-based semantic search finds relevant trials without exact medical terms
3Check the hard rulesRule-based filters confirm age, location and recruitment status

Product decisions

  • MVP on one therapy area and one geography
  • Accuracy and explanation over volume of matches
  • Clinical screening stays human; sharing needs consent

How it's measured

  • Precision@5 target of 80% or more
  • Trial discovery from ~30 minutes to under 5
  • Qualified referrals, not clicks
